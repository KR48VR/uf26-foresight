Tsinghua University · Scratch rebuild · September 2026

Tsinghua is being asked to become China’s education-AI utility. That is not the same job as remaining a university.

Li Luming told the Class of 2026 to keep cognitive sovereignty and still learn to code. The organisation behind the sentence is already shipping knowledge engines to more than a hundred other campuses. The hard problem is whether Qinghuayuan still trains people who can refuse a model, or only people who can distribute one.

Board two-pager

External reconstruction for a president and council elsewhere. Not a document that could be tabled, unedited, in Beijing.

Crux

Keep Tsinghua a place that certifies judgment the model cannot sit — programming, experiment, clinic, design studio — while the state is using it as the operating system for “AI + education” across China, export controls ration compute, and international co-authorship is politically expensive.

Diagnosis in six lines

Tsinghua already won the domestic prestige and paper-volume contest. End-2025 it enrolled 63,132 students, including 16,526 undergraduates and 24,854 doctoral candidates. In August 2026 it took more than 4,000 new undergraduates, 57 percent of them into fifteen residential shuyuan. From eight AI-enabled course pilots in autumn 2023 it reached 451 courses by spring 2026 and is sharing discipline knowledge engines — integrated circuits, industrial engineering, environment — with on the order of eighty to 130 partner universities. The April 2026 national AI + Education Action Plan wants a “smart education” system by 2030; Tsinghua is writing the standards and the content. Li’s graduation brief was the opposite sentence: students must still learn programming; humanities must calibrate the model; “cognitive sovereignty” is the point. Those two jobs fight. One produces a scarce graduate. The other produces a national platform. Export controls and a thinning two-facing faculty market decide how much of the science remains citable outside the wall.

Guiding policy

Treat the national knowledge-engine role as a service line, not the definition of a Tsinghua degree. Spend presidential attention on three things that do not scale by licence: which assessments a model is forbidden to sit; which collaborations stay open enough that a paper is still treated as science abroad; and whether the fifteen shuyuan actually change how a 19-year-old is examined, or only where they sleep.

Coherent actions

  1. Publish a short list of programmes where a closed-book human still sits the binding assessment — code written in the room, wet lab, clinic, design jury, oral. Count those programmes annually. Do not count “AI-enabled courses.”
  2. Separate the knowledge-engine franchise from degree design. The 130-university sharing programme can grow. It must not set the standard for what a Tsinghua bachelor is.
  3. Keep a switchable compute and model stack. Sovereign models and open-weight Chinese systems are the hedge. A single-vendor campus AI is a dependency the export office can break.
  4. Protect a thin two-facing corridor: Schwarzman College, selected labs, and a published co-authorship rule that still lets a Tsinghua paper be hired against in Boston, Zurich, or Singapore.

Start

A short list of programmes where a closed-book human still sits the binding assessment, counted annually. A switchable compute stack. A published two-facing co-authorship rule.

Stop

Letting the 130-university knowledge-engine set the bachelor standard. Single-vendor campus AI. Platform-as-transcript.

Go

Two-facing talent as the hedge. Lab intensity. Adapt the franchise; keep the exam that still sorts.

Full report

What Tsinghua is

Tsinghua is the state’s preferred comprehensive science-and-engineering university, with a humanities and social-science layer that exists to serve the science project and the cadre pipeline. As of 31 December 2025 it reported 33 schools, 54 departments, 17,382 faculty and staff, and 63,132 registered students. The 2026 undergraduate intake exceeded 4,000: about 3,800 from the mainland, eighty-plus from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, and three hundred-plus internationals from 45 countries, most of them Belt-and-Road. The Strong Foundation Plan took nearly 900 into five shuyuan. Special and national rural plans took 555 from more than 260 county schools.

The 2025–26 public monuments are easy to list. An AI Agent Hospital was inaugurated in April 2025. Li Luming spent 2026 on the road — Singapore, Japan, New Zealand, Switzerland, Germany — selling Tsinghua as an education-and-science partner that can still sign memoranda. Qiu Yong, as Party secretary, took the civilisational brief to France and Greece. Internally the university ran a series of “talent in the intelligent era” seminars whose consensus Li quoted at graduation: large models already programme well; Tsinghua students must still learn to programme, then grow into people who can understand, steer, and lead the models. The Institute for International AI Governance, an AI Open Alliance, and a national research network on AI development and security are the governance layer. The knowledge-engine franchise is the scale layer. Neither layer is the undergraduate degree.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • The state wants a utility. The April 2026 AI + Education Action Plan aims at a smart-education system by 2030. Tsinghua is already the reference implementation: 451 AI-enabled courses, national standards work, knowledge engines shared outward. That is a real service to the sector. It is also a temptation to define success as distribution volume.
  • Export controls and compute. Frontier training is rationed. Open-weight Chinese models and sovereign compute are the available hedge. A research university that cannot train or fine-tune at the frontier becomes a fine-tuning shop on other people’s weights.
  • Two-facing talent is thinning. The best doctoral students and a slice of faculty still want a career that is hireable in more than one system. Visa politics, security reviews, and gift politics all tax that corridor. Schwarzman College is a symbol of it, not a substitute for lab-level co-authorship.
  • Shuyuan reform is unfinished. Fifteen undergraduate colleges now take 57 percent of the new class. The question is whether a shuyuan changes the exam or only the residential brand. A college that does not own assessment is a dormitory.
  • Paper volume is no longer scarce. CSRankings and Nature Index still move careers inside China. They do not, by themselves, keep a Tsinghua paper treatable as science in a lab that has stopped co-authoring.
“Embrace change and lead the AI era.” Stand firm in cognitive sovereignty. Students must still learn to programme. — Li Luming, address to the Class of 2026

Crux

The addressable difficulty is this: stop the knowledge-engine franchise and the national-standards role from eating the definition of a Tsinghua degree, while still taking the state’s assignment and still keeping a thin corridor of internationally legible science. A new institute does not touch it. A ChatGPT-class licence does not touch it. A published list of model-forbidden assessments, a separate P&L for the franchise, and a co-authorship rule that faculty will actually use do.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not Tsinghua’s published plan

Diagnosis

Tsinghua solved “look like a global STEM university inside a Party-led system.” The remaining problem is product definition: a flagship being pulled into becoming the country’s education-AI operating system, under compute rationing, with a two-facing faculty market that is getting more expensive to keep.

Guiding policy

Protect scarce human certification as the brand. Let the knowledge engines be a service line. Take sovereign-AI money that does not buy the exam. Keep a published, switchable model stack. Spend shuyuan reform on assessment, not architecture.

Coherent actions

Annual public count of model-forbidden binding assessments. Franchise separated from degree design. Switchable compute. A thin two-facing corridor with a co-authorship rule. No new “AI college” announcement until three existing shuyuan have changed how they examine.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — International scientific legibility. Tsinghua work stays co-authorable, citable, and hireable in more than one system, or it becomes national infrastructure that foreign labs treat as a parallel literature.

Axis B — What the degree certifies. A Tsinghua bachelor still signals judgment a model cannot sit, or the degree becomes a receipt for having trained on the same engines the university sold to the rest of the sector.

Implications

The Party Committee does not need to pick a world. It needs a policy that is not ruined by three of the four. Model-forbidden assessments and a separated franchise help in every world except “Ministry of Models,” where nothing the campus does is the binding constraint. Schwarzman and the governance institutes help most in “two-facing peak” and “sovereign cloister.” They are wasted motion in “export bureau” unless they come with exclusive data and harder exams.

A bad strategy would be: announce another AI college, count 451 enabled courses as reform, leave the binding exam as it was in 2019 with an honour-code paragraph, and let the knowledge-engine KPI do the rest.

For students · Tsinghua

The engine will pass the homework. It will not sit the exam that still matters — if your programme still has one.

You were admitted to a university that is being asked to educate China at scale. That is a service. It is not automatically your education. Choose the room that still makes you do the thing a model cannot do in the room.

Best case for you

Two-facing peak

Harder exams. Real labs. A transcript that still sorts you from a prompt notebook. Worth the hours.

Comfortable trap

Export bureau

Everything is assisted. Your degree looks like the course pack Tsinghua sold to 130 other campuses. Employers start asking what you did with the camera off.

Narrow excellence

Sovereign cloister

Fewer overseas doors. Deeper domestic and security-cleared ones. If you wanted a two-passport career, plan the second door yourself.

Worst case

Ministry of Models

You collected a brand. The scarce jobs went to people who built things outside the shuyuan.

Do this while you are here

  • Start Collect at least one piece of work per term that bans the model in the room. If your course has none, that is information about the course.
  • Go Use the knowledge engines as instruments, not as a substitute for a department that still marks hard.
  • Go Keep a working relationship in a second system. Tsinghua’s value was two-facing. So is yours.
  • Start If you are in a shuyuan, ask who owns your binding assessment. If the answer is “the department, as before,” you are in a dormitory with a better name.
  • Stop Stop counting AI-enabled courses as the exam. Sit the one the engine cannot.
