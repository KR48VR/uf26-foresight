Board two-pager

External reconstruction. Not a document that could be tabled, unedited, in Beijing or the Shanghai Municipal Government.

Crux

Keep Fudan a comprehensive university that certifies clinical, social-science and basic-science judgment — while the city and the state are using it as the diplomatic front end of China’s global AI-governance offer to the Global South.

Diagnosis in six lines

Fudan is Shanghai’s comprehensive flagship: medicine, humanities, social sciences, and a science layer that has never been Tsinghua. That mix is the asset. In 2025 the university stood up the Center for Global AI Innovative Governance with municipal backing, Jin Li as director, Chen Zhimin as executive director. UN digital envoys walked the campus. In 2026 Jin took “Lego thinking” to the NPC: stop expanding discipline boxes, start assembling them against national tasks; he says 80 percent of Fudan’s key focus areas are already interdisciplinary. Shanghai Forum 2026 ran under “The Age of Reconfiguration.” Xi’s first WAIC appearance since 2018 and the launch of WAICO in Shanghai pulled Fudan further into the city’s AI-diplomacy stack. Yao Xu of the same centre described WAICO as an institutional circle of friends for the Global South. That is a real foreign-policy assignment. It is also how a university becomes a protocol office with hospitals attached.

Guiding policy

Treat the governance centre as a service line to the city and the ministry, not the definition of a Fudan degree. Spend presidential attention on three things communiqués cannot replace: clinical and social-science assessments a model cannot sit; which “Lego” assemblies actually change what a 20-year-old is examined on; and a two-facing research corridor that is not only Global-South training modules.

Coherent actions

  1. Publish a list of medical, law, economics and basic-science programmes whose binding assessment remains unassisted. Count them annually. Do not count training modules delivered to visiting officials.
  2. Give every Lego-style interdisciplinary structure an exam owner. A structure without an exam is a slide.
  3. Keep a Western and a Global-South research corridor as two separate books. One book is not a two-facing university.
  4. Cap the share of presidential time spent on WAICO/WAIC protocol. A president who is only a diplomat has already chosen the world.

Start

An annual count of medical, law, economics and basic-science programmes whose binding assessment remains unassisted. Every Lego structure gets an exam owner.

Stop

Counting training modules as exams. Presidential time spent only on WAICO protocol. One research corridor pretending to be two-facing.

Go

Handan clinics and seminars. Two separate books for Western and Global-South corridors. Adapt the forum; keep the clinic.

Full report

What Fudan is

Fudan is a comprehensive research university in Shanghai, founded 1905, with a medical school that is part of the city’s clinical system and a social-science and humanities layer that SJTU does not have. Jin Li, a population geneticist and CAS academician, has been president since November 2021. His public method in 2026 is “Lego thinking”: treat disciplines as pieces, assemble them against national and municipal tasks, stop growing boxes. About 80 percent of the university’s key focus areas, he says, already cut across disciplines and line up with central, ministry, and Shanghai plans.

The 2025–26 public monuments are the governance centre, UN visits, Shanghai Forum, and Fudan’s role in the WAICO season. Those are the city’s preferred story. The undergraduate and clinical degree is a different story, and it is the one employers and foreign faculties still have to trust.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Shanghai’s AI-diplomacy assignment. WAIC, WAICO, municipal money, UN traffic. Fudan is the academic face. Faces get used.
  • SJTU next door. Ding Kuiling’s campus is the engineering-and-industrial-AI competitor. Fudan cannot win that contest and should not try. Its difference is medicine plus argument.
  • Lego reform as a two-edged tool. Assembling pieces against national tasks can sharpen a university. It can also dissolve the faculties that still know how to examine.
  • Global South as a market and a mission. Training modules, capacity-building, open-weight politics. Useful. Not a substitute for a Fudan bachelor or an MD that a hospital will still trust.
“A ‘Lego-style’ academic system will allow limited components to perform multiple tasks, avoiding the bloated expansion of disciplines.” — Jin Li, NPC sidelines, March 2026

Crux

Stop the governance-diplomacy franchise from eating the definition of a Fudan degree, and make Lego assemblies own exams rather than slides — while still taking the city’s WAICO assignment. A new centre does not touch it. A published assessment list and a split calendar do.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not Fudan’s published plan

Diagnosis

Fudan solved “comprehensive Shanghai flagship.” The remaining problem is product definition: a university being pulled into becoming the protocol layer of China’s AI-governance offer, under a city that already has an engineering champion next door.

Guiding policy

Protect clinical and argumentative certification as the brand. Let the governance centre be a service line. Make every Lego structure own an exam. Keep two international books, not one.

Coherent actions

Annual count of model-forbidden clinical and social-science assessments. Exam owner for every Lego structure. Split Western / Global-South research books. Cap protocol time on the presidential calendar.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Shanghai as an open research city. People, data and co-authorship still move through more than one system, or the city tightens into a national-showcase campus.

Axis B — What the degree certifies. A Fudan credential still signals clinic and argument, or it becomes a membership card for the governance campus.

A open · B scarce

Handan republic

2030

Medicine and social sciences still examine unassisted: a clinical viva, an unseen argument, a proof. The governance centre hosts UN visitors and does not set the undergraduate exam. Every Lego-style structure has a named exam owner. Jin’s public calendar is still mostly campus.

2040

Fudan is the two-facing comprehensive Shanghai actually needs. SJTU can keep industrial AI. A hospital still trusts a Fudan MD; a foreign faculty still reads a Fudan economist. WAICO is a service line, not the front door.

A open · B cheap

Protocol hotel

2030

Forums and modules travel. The undergraduate degree is a prestige wrapper. Hospitals quietly re-examine their own hires because the clinical assessment has become a literacy badge. Students appear in WAIC photographs and cannot sit an unseen paper.

2040

The valuable Fudan is the centre, the forum, and the municipal relationships. The bachelor is hospitality. Foreign partners come for the protocol calendar and skip the transcript.

A tight · B scarce

Municipal cloister

2030

One bloc thins. The degree is scarce because clinical data and local questions cannot be offshored. A student who wanted a two-passport career leaves earlier, or never applies. Shanghai still uses Fudan as a civic instrument.

2040

Excellent and less internationally legible. Talent that wanted a two-passport career leaves earlier. Fudan is a superb municipal university with a world memory. The governance franchise is domestic.

A tight · B cheap

WAICO college

2030

Both uniqueness and openness go. Fudan administers national modules and lets the brand tour. Lego structures own brochures. The president’s calendar is a diplomatic circuit.

2040

Research concentrates in a few institutes and hospitals. The university is a hospitality layer over a municipal science-and-diplomacy agency. The 1905 name remains. The comprehensive degree does not.

Implications

Jin does not need to pick a world. He needs a policy that survives three of the four. Exam ownership and a split calendar help everywhere except “WAICO college.” The governance centre helps in “Handan republic” only if it stays a side door.

A bad strategy would be: announce another Global-South training cycle, leave the medical exam as it was in 2019 with an AI-literacy paragraph, and let Shanghai Forum do the rest.

For students · Fudan

The forum will put you in a photograph. It will not sit your clinic or your argument.

You were admitted to a comprehensive university that is being asked to host the city’s AI diplomacy. That is a service. It is not automatically your education. Choose the room that still makes you do the thing a model cannot do.

Best case for you

Handan republic

Harder clinics and seminars. A transcript that still sorts you.

Comfortable trap

Protocol hotel

Everything is assisted and photographed. Employers start asking what you did with the camera off.

Narrow excellence

Municipal cloister

Fewer overseas doors. Deeper Shanghai ones. Plan the second door yourself.

Worst case

WAICO college

You collected a brand. The scarce jobs went to people who built things outside the forum.

Do this while you are here

  • Start Collect one unassisted clinical or argumentative piece per term. If your programme has none, that is information.
  • Start Ask who owns the exam in any “Lego” structure you join. No owner means no degree, only a slide.
  • Go Use the governance centre as an instrument, not a substitute for a department that still marks hard.
  • Go Keep a working relationship in a second system. Fudan’s value was two-facing. So is yours.
  • Stop Stop treating the forum photograph as the clinic. Own an exam.
