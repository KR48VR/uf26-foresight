SNU can become Korea’s physical-AI and intelligent-medicine university — or a ChatGPT-Edu campus that the regions already resent.

Board two-pager

For the President and Board of Trustees

Crux

Turn “AI-Native Campus” from forty-seven tasks and a site licence into a national lab-adjacent franchise — physical AI, semiconductors, intelligent connected care — without becoming a Seoul-only elite the regions punish, and without letting NVIDIA or OpenAI set the research agenda.

Diagnosis in six lines

Seoul National University is the republic’s flagship, with the privileges and the political target that come with that. ChatGPT Edu went live for all members in June 2026. SNU joined OpenAI’s NextGenAI consortium. An undergraduate AI class of 30 started in the second semester of 2026; an AI graduate school is slated for March 2027. In July 2026 SNU and NVIDIA announced Korea’s first multi-year NVAITC joint lab, aimed at physical AI and a sovereign-AI testbed on the Siheung campus. SNU Hospital launched SNUH.AI and an intelligent connected-care story. That is a coherent industrial direction. It is not yet a university redesign. Flagships die by accumulating partnerships while undergraduate education and regional legitimacy stay in 2015.

Guiding policy

Physical AI and connected care as the two problems SNU is willing to be judged on. OpenAI and NVIDIA as instruments, not identity. Siheung as a real second scientific city, not a relief valve.

Coherent actions

  1. Collapse the 47 AI-Native tasks into five board-level outcomes: physical-AI prototypes, sovereign-stack hours at Siheung, AI+X graduates placed in Korean industry, SNUH.AI clinical deployments, and unaided student performance that does not fall.
  2. Build the 2027 AI graduate school as a joint faculty with engineering, medicine and public administration — not a new silo that raids CS.
  3. Put the NVIDIA lab on a public problem list with Korean industrial counterparts named.
  4. Use Siheung for compute, clinic and civic deliberation, not only overflow teaching.

Full report

What SNU is

Korea’s defining comprehensive flagship — Gwanak as the academic core, Siheung as the expansion joint, Seoul National University Hospital as the clinical empire. In 2026 the university tried to re-describe itself as an AI-Native Campus. The Presidential Committee for a Basic Society used Siheung in September 2026 as the venue for a 200-citizen deliberation on jobs and income under AI. That last fact is the political weather the flagship now sails in.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Flagship legitimacy. SNU is the exam-system prize and the object of regional grievance.
  • Sovereign AI industrial policy. NVIDIA at Siheung is how a university becomes part of the national stack — or how it rents a logo.
  • Hospital as the other university. If SNUH.AI and the Gwanak AI school do not share people and problems, SNU has two AI strategies and no kernel.
  • Tool access is the easy layer. ChatGPT Edu removes the subscription divide. It does not redesign assessment or doctoral training.
“A meaningful turning point for SNU to become a global AI research hub.” — Honglim Ryu, on the NVIDIA NVAITC lab, 24 July 2026

Crux

Focus. Name two or three problems the republic will still credit SNU for in 2035 — physical AI at Siheung, connected care with SNUH, a public-service class that can govern AI — and resource those. Programme count is running ahead of problem choice.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not the published plan

Diagnosis

The partnerships are real and early. The operating system of the flagship has not been rebuilt to match.

Guiding policy

Two hard problems, one public-trust constraint, vendors as tools. Shrink the AI-Native list until a trustee can recite it.

Coherent actions

Five outcome metrics. Joint AI graduate faculty with medicine and public administration. Named industrial problems for the NVIDIA lab. Siheung as compute-clinic-civic campus. Protect unaided cores.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Which AI SNU actually owns. Physical AI, chips, robotics, hospital systems — or a general LLM campus that looks like everywhere else with better exam scores.

Axis B — What the flagship is allowed to be. A trusted national instrument other Korean universities can use, or a politicised Seoul elite that funds get steered away from.

Physical AI · Politicised

Gated Laboratory

2030

The NVIDIA lab works. Access is narrow. Regions treat SNU as a private club with public land. Siheung compute hours are real and rationed to a few labs. A student who is not already inside the right group will spend four years on the chat layer.

2040

Strong labs, weak licence. A future government relocates missions to KAIST or POSTECH because the flagship could not share. Gwanak remains first in the suneung hierarchy and second in the industrial one.

LLM campus · Politicised

The Resented Portal

Implications

The bad strategy is treating NVIDIA, OpenAI, SNUH.AI and the AI graduate school as four press cycles. The robust strategy is two problems, five metrics, and Siheung as a scientific city that a citizen panel could walk through and recognise.
