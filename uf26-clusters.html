A university can sit in more than one group — that is the point, not a defect. Hits almost everyone

Four forces that are not a cluster

These are the climate. Clusters are how each university is exposed to the climate.

  1. Cheap generative tools flatten the average essay, case, code drop and portfolio. The degree only stays scarce if the exam still requires a human in the room, a bench, a clinic, or a job that can reject the work.
  2. Who pays for discovery is moving — state, firm, tuition, philanthropy. The payer starts writing the problem list unless the university has a written rule that says otherwise.
  3. Talent still has to cross a border, and that border is thickening: visas, security clearances, two-facing science, domestic-only pipelines.
  4. Rank is a costume if the exam, the lab slot, or the work term does not change. QS can rise while the scarce product dies.

Cut A — What the degree must still prove

Best for students. Groups universities by the scarce thing the credential is supposed to certify after the tool is cheap.

Judgment room

Oral, studio, tutorial, seminar

NUS · Oxford · Cambridge · SMU · SUTD · Olin · RCA · LSE · UAS · Beida

The product is a person who can still be failed in a room. If the final is a finished artefact a model can draft, the cluster collapses into the same cheap credential.

Job / practice as the exam

Someone outside the university grades the work

SIT · SUSS · IMD · INSEAD

The scarce proof is a firm, a clinic, a P&L, or a peer room. Content libraries will eat these places if the third-year job or the sitting-manager cohort becomes ceremonial.

Doctoral / lab production slot

A funded person attached to an unowned question

MIT · Caltech · Harvard · Stanford · Imperial

The unit of production is not the undergraduate lecture. It is a doctoral student or a measurement that still has no customer. Industry money can keep the lights on and still kill the unit.

Scale flagship that must stay scarce

Judgement at volume, or a ranked crowd

NTU · UC · SNU · Tsinghua · Zhejiang

These places can fill seats from rank. The crux is whether a minority of programmes still sort people after AI, or the whole degree becomes a well-branded crowd.

Integration machine

Two floors on one ledger

Penn · CMU · SJTU

The scarce product is a person who can walk a hospital and a lab, or a robot and a play, in the same week. Each school winning separately is the failure mode.

Cut B — Who writes the problem list

Best for boards. Groups universities by the payer or political weather most likely to set the question.

Washington bargain

Federal science + endowment tax + visa weather

MIT · Harvard · Caltech · Stanford · Penn · UC

The first fight is public. The slow fight is labs that do not restock and international doctoral yield that does not return. Industry money is the tempting substitute.

Ministry / municipal utility

The state wants a platform, not only a campus

Tsinghua · Beida · Fudan · Zhejiang · SJTU · SNU

National AI-education utilities, municipal industry policy, and flagship resentment are the weather. The university function is what has to be protected inside the mission.

Firm / Valley annex

The neighbour can hire the faculty and set the brief

Stanford · CMU · SIT · Imperial · Zhejiang

Proximity is an asset until dissertations read like internships and capstones become tours. Industry may fund platforms. It should not buy the question.

City-state compact

One operating system, seven different jobs

NUS · NTU · SMU · SUSS · SIT · UAS · SUTD

MOE weather, SkillsFuture, and a small labour market. The cluster dies if all seven imitate the flagship pair.

Multi-campus market

The room is the product; campuses are not three schools

INSEAD · IMD

AI content and online scale attack the only scarce good: a peer room of sitting managers or a one-year cohort that does not agree with itself.

Small-room method vs scale

Growth is how the charter dies

SMU · SUTD · Olin · RCA · IMD · Caltech

The ratio is the product. A second campus of lectures, a graduate programme for prestige, or a ranking-play master’s is the same failure written four ways.

Cut C — Shared weather systems

Best as a site index. Five operating environments. Geography shows up here because weather is regional — but the groups are not “Asia / West.”

C1

Singapore operating system

All seven. One ministry, one labour market, seven distinct charters. Forecast any one of them without the other six and you will miss the substitution effect (if NUS becomes a ranked distributor, SUTD and SMU either become more scarce or get flattened with it).

C2

US federal bargain + industry annex

MIT · Stanford · Caltech · Harvard · Penn · CMU. Public variants: UC, UIUC. Shared shock: awards, endowment tax, visas, and firms that can now run the frontier without a campus.

C3

UK fee + national-lab + collegiate room

Oxford · Cambridge · Imperial · LSE · RCA. Overseas-fee exposure, UK industrial strategy, and a teaching method (tutorial, supervision, critique, argument) that AI makes either more valuable or merely heritage.

C4

China two-facing vs utility — HKUST as the hinge

Beida · Tsinghua · Fudan · Zhejiang · SJTU · HKUST. The shared question: remain a university that examines judgment, or become the education-AI / municipal / Bay-area delivery arm. HKUST is the two-facing test case.

C5

Boutique rooms

INSEAD · IMD · Olin · SUTD · RCA. Small on purpose. Copied by larger neighbours. Growth, a content studio, or a baby-research-university move erases the charter.

How this is set up for a later forecasting layer

Each pack already has four named worlds and watchpoints (observables, not hopes). That is enough structure to attach priors later without rewriting the briefs.

  • Unit of forecast: a world on one university (e.g. NUS · Ranked Distributor by 2030), not a vibe about “higher education.”
  • Prior: start uninformative, or start from the board two-pager’s implied base rate. Do not print fake precision now.
  • Likelihood: the watchpoints already in the pack — exam format actually changed, doctoral yield, firm hire-off-capstone, visa/grant line, studio ratio, alliance conferral.
  • Update cousins, not the roster: Cut A / B / C are the correlation keys. Evidence that MIT became a Sponsored Institute should move Stanford · The Annex and CMU · Task Order U more than it moves SUSS or RCA.
  • What this page is not: a model, a dashboard, or a score. Bayes and CoS can sit on top of these frames when you decide to turn the site into a forecasting engine. The frames stay; the numbers come later.

If two cuts disagree about a university, keep both labels. Disagreement is information about the crux, not a data-cleaning problem.
