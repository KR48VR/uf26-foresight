Board two-pager

For the President and Council

Crux

Prove that a postgraduate art-and-design college still mints taste and authorship after generative tools flood the portfolio — while paying for a seven-year heritage renovation and leaving White City without shrinking the studio.

Diagnosis in six lines

The RCA is a postgraduate specialist whose ranking is a habit and whose economics are not. Lindner’s 2026–30 plan aims at the 200th anniversary in 2037: a global institution, hubs outside the UK, two central London campuses after White City closes. Kensington works began August 2025. Witherford Watson Mann’s Darwin scheme is phased to 2032 at about £90 million. That is a lot of noise and cash for a college of fewer than three thousand. Meanwhile junior creative labour is the first labour generative tools cheapen. Lindner has said he fears geopolitics more than AI, and that AI is a tool the RCA should help shape — philosophy and art inside the package, not only computer science. AiDLab with Hong Kong PolyU is in a second phase on fashion and textiles. The February 2026 AI Festival was the right instinct. A festival is not a critique standard. If the degree still accepts a portfolio a mid model can pastiche, QS will lag reality by about five years and then fall off a cliff.

Guiding policy

Studio density first; campuses as the means. Treat generative tools as material to be authored against, not as a curriculum chapter. Finish the two-campus consolidation without using renovation as an excuse to thin contact time. Keep the Hong Kong lab and any future overseas hub on a short leash: they are instruments, not an escape from London costs.

Coherent actions

  1. Publish a college-wide authorship standard: what must be made, defended live, and un-generatable in each programme. Show work, not statements.
  2. A studio-hour floor through the Kensington works and the White City exit. If hours fall, the plan has already failed.
  3. Price the £90 million Darwin phasing against student numbers. Do not grow headcount to pay for heritage if the studio cannot hold the growth.
  4. Put AiDLab and the new future-technologies institute on the same critique as Fashion and Sculpture. Tools without taste are Imperial’s problem, not yours — unless you copy them.

Start

A published studio-ratio cap. An AI making protocol: what must be on the desk and defended in critique.

Stop

More seats, thinner desks. Sponsor briefs as the only project list. A lecture hall as growth strategy.

Go

The critique that can still fail work. Studio intensity. Adapt the tools; keep the desk.

Full report

What the RCA is

A postgraduate art-and-design college that became the reference brand for taste in the English-speaking world. LASALLE and UAL and RISD produce volume. The RCA produces a certain kind of author. That claim is only as good as the studio.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Generative slop in junior creative labour. The first years of commercial design, illustration, and motion are being repriced. A master’s that does not change what it certifies becomes a finishing school for a shrinking middle.
  • Campus physics. Three sites now; two from August 2027; a listed building under scaffolding until 2032. Logistics can destroy a studio culture without anyone meaning to.
  • UK arts-school economics. Levy, visas, frozen public money. Specialist providers get a 220-student levy allowance. The RCA is larger than that allowance and smaller than Imperial’s shock-absorber.
  • Geopolitics over tools. Lindner is right that the bigger threat is the movement of people and the mood around China-facing labs. AiDLab is both an asset and an exposure.
“It’s not just computer science and engineering. You need a kind of creativity, you need philosophy, you need the arts.” — Christoph Lindner, 2025

Crux

Keep authorship scarce while the buildings move and the tools cheapen the first draft. Hours, live critique, and a refusal to grow headcount to pay for stone are the addressable moves. An AI festival is not.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not the RCA’s published plan

Diagnosis

The RCA is a taste engine in a cost squeeze, running a seven-year building project at the exact moment generative tools attack the junior end of its professions.

Guiding policy

Studio floor first. Authorship standard in public. Renovation sized to the college, not the other way around. Overseas labs as instruments.

Coherent actions

Live-defence assessment. Studio-hour floor through 2032. Headcount cap tied to space. AiDLab inside the same critique culture. No third campus story until the second one works.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Human authorship remains scarce and valued, or generative production collapses junior creative labour and the master’s premium with it.

Axis B — London studio density plus global intake holds, or the RCA becomes a smaller UK arts school with a famous name.

A scarce · B global

Albertopolis atelier

2030

Battersea and Kensington are both working studios. White City has closed on time without losing a programme. A fashion or animation student still defends a major piece live each term, with process files on the table and the generative tool named rather than hidden. International yield in Fashion, Animation and Design Products holds. Darwin scaffolding has not eaten contact hours.

2040

Still the reference postgraduate for taste in the English-speaking world. Smaller than the 2010s fantasy of infinite growth, more distinct because the crit got harder as junior commercial work got cheaper. A studio in Seoul or Brooklyn still hires an RCA graduate for authorship, not for software speed.

A scarce · B smaller

National conservatoire

2030

Taste holds. Passports thin: levy and visas cut the overseas master’s that paid for the studio. More British, more public-mission — a civic arts school the DCMS can point at. Hours are protected; the room is less global. A student who wanted a two-city career starts arranging the second city themselves.

2040

Excellent and less internationally legible. A civic arts school with a world memory. RISD and a couple of Asian postgraduates take the students who used to treat London as default. The Darwin building is beautiful. The network is narrower.

A slop · B global

Brand workshop

2030

Students still come because QS says so. Portfolios converge on the same model-fluent look. Industry interviews for software speed and prompt craft; the live crit becomes a formality. Hours look fine on a spreadsheet and thin in the room because renovation logistics keep moving people.

2040

A prestigious wrapper around tools everyone else has. The RCA brand still opens a first meeting. It does not sort a second one. Junior creative labour has been repriced; the master’s premium went with it.

A slop · B smaller

Listed souvenir

2030

Buildings improve on schedule. The degree does not. A student can complete a programme as a polished generative portfolio plus three weeks of studio theatre. White City exits late and a small programme is quietly folded to pay for stone.

2040

A beautiful campus that used to be a college. Tourists photograph Darwin. Hiring panels treat the MA as a finishing year, not as authorship. UAL and a handful of European schools took the work that used to be RCA’s.

Implications

Studio hours and live defence help in every world except “listed souvenir.” Growing headcount to pay for Darwin helps only the contractor. Designed smaller is better than a full campus with an empty crit.

A bad strategy would be: an AI festival every February, a headcount target to fund stone, and critique that never asks to see the work without the tool.

For students · RCA

If the crit can be faked, the degree already has been.

The buildings will be noisy. The tools will be fluent. Your only scarce asset here is a way of making that still looks like someone. Defend the work live. Keep the files that show the decisions.

Best case for you

Albertopolis atelier

Crits get harder on purpose. You defend the work live, with the process files on the table. The name still sorts you because the room can still fail you.

Narrower, more serious

National conservatoire

Fewer overseas doors. Deeper British ones — White City tenants, UK industrial briefs, a smaller international cohort. If you wanted a two-passport practice, build the second door yourself while you are enrolled.

Comfortable trap

Brand workshop

The shops are beautiful. The portfolios look like everyone else’s Midjourney week. At interview they ask what you made with the tool off. The postcode does not answer that.

Worst case

Listed souvenir

You rented a famous building. The practice moved. Keep a trail of decisions and one collaborator outside London before you need them.

Do this while you are here

  • Start Defend at least one major piece per term with the model off and the process on the table.
  • Go Protect studio hours as if they were the degree. They are.
  • Go Use AiDLab if it serves a question you own. Do not confuse a lab brand with authorship.
  • Go Keep one serious collaborator outside London. The RCA’s magnetism is not a law.
  • Start Photograph the process, not only the object. If the portfolio is only finished images, you used RCA as a shop class.
  • Stop Stop building a portfolio of only finished images. The process file is the degree.
