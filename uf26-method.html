How each pack is built

Book A is Rumelt, Good Strategy/Bad Strategy (2011). Book B is Rumelt, The Crux (2022). Scenarios are a 2x2 on two critical uncertainties, written at 2030 and again at 2040.

The kernel

  1. Diagnosis. The pattern, not the press release.
  2. Guiding policy. The approach for resolving the crux. Not a motto.
  3. Coherent actions. A short set of moves that only make sense together.

The crux

The most important difficulty that is also addressable from the president's chair in the next few years.
