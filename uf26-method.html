How each pack is built

Rumelt kernel (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions) plus a 2×2 on two critical uncertainties, written at 2030 and 2040. The kernel is a reconstruction, not the university’s published strategy.

  1. Diagnosis — the pattern, not the press release.
  2. Guiding policy — how to resolve the crux. Not a motto.
  3. Coherent actions — a short set of moves that only make sense together.

The crux is the most important difficulty that is still addressable from the president’s chair.
