Board two-pager

For the President and Board of Trustees

Crux

Stay small enough to design. Grow by deepening Design·AI into firms and government, not by adding generic headcount. Growing like a third engineering school would erase the only reason SUTD exists beside NTU.

Diagnosis in six lines

SUTD is the only Singapore university that can honestly say design was in the charter before AI was in the brochure. LEAP (2024) made Design·AI the growth strategy. The DAI degree now carries social-science tracks. Phoon wants “trilinguals”: domain, AI, design. Team-based, design-first pedagogy is still rare in Asia. Multi-model access is a sharper AI stance than NUS’s OpenAI-centric deal or NTU’s Google suite. The risk is complementary: SUTD can become a boutique that industry loves and the Ministry finds expensive, or it can accept growth targets that turn capstone into lecture. MIT was the founding template. That shadow is now a constraint.

Guiding policy

Protect studio time as the scarce asset. Export the method through DIVE, LEAP placements and executive Design·AI, not through a second campus of lectures. Make the Gateway a pedagogy of choose, compare and refuse.

Coherent actions

  1. Cap undergraduate growth. Export the method, do not lecture it at scale.
  2. Make the LLM Gateway a pedagogy: students must choose, compare and refuse models, not just prompt one.
  3. Keep DAI’s social-science tracks staffed with real social scientists, or drop the claim.
  4. Put faculty into industry and industry into curriculum on a named quota.
  5. Measure graduate work by shipped artefacts and judgement under uncertainty, not by starting salary alone.

Full report

What it is

SUTD is Singapore’s design-technology boutique, founded with MIT, now run as a Design·AI university under Phoon. Pillars: Architecture and Sustainable Design, Engineering Product Development, Engineering Systems Design, Computer Science and Design, Design and AI. East Coast campus. Small cohorts on purpose.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Generative design tools that flatten the old studio advantage unless judgement is taught harder.
  • National appetite for more STEM seats that does not automatically want another SUTD.
  • Multi-model world matching Singapore’s hub posture — US and Chinese systems on one gateway.
  • Competition from NUS CDE and NTU CCDS at larger scale.
“Our students will work with many different AI models over their careers, and the models will keep changing.” — Chee Yeow Meng, 14 September 2026

Crux

SUTD’s scarce problem is how to remain a studio when every larger neighbour can buy the same models.

Studio holds · profession

Trilingual Guild

2030

A student can design, code, and explain the user or city they just altered. Firms send problems because the section is small enough to use. Undergraduate intake and studio section size are published caps, not aspirations. One Design·AI clinic runs live public problems; the extra AI-branded minors have been killed.

2040

SUTD is a guild, not a miniature NTU. Graduates are scarce and priced that way. The MIT founding story is a method still visible in the timetable, not nostalgia.

Studio holds · slogan

Beloved Boutique

2030

Everyone who knows SUTD loves it. Headcount is tiny. National conversation still runs through NUS and NTU. Studio ratios hold; leverage does not. Gateway comparisons remain a habit among the few employers who already know the school.

2040

A cherished specialist with less leverage than its teaching deserves. Excellent for the students who found it. Invisible to the rest.

Growth · profession

Third Engineering School

2030

To look bigger, SUTD adds conventional engineering volume. Studio hours fall. The tutor no longer knows every student’s work. Design·AI is an extra minor on a standard engineering degree.

2040

A third engineering school with a design myth in the prospectus. Employers cannot tell a SUTD graduate from any other AI-minor engineer.

Growth · slogan

Diluted Charter

2030

‘Design·AI’ is on every page. Capstones are prompted decks. Intake grew to look national. The ratio that was the product is gone.

2040

The founding story is a museum. Employers cannot tell a SUTD graduate from any other AI-minor engineer. Changi is a small campus with a large slogan.

Implications

The Board should treat undergraduate headcount as a threat indicator. The growth path is executive education, firm-embedded studios and a graduate Design·AI school — not another thousand freshmen.

A bad strategy would be: take a growth target, pick one vendor for convenience, and let DAI become a computing minor with a design sticker.

For students · SUTD

You are supposed to be trilingual. Most people will only be bilingual.

SUTD is the small design-and-technology university in Changi. “Trilingual” here means you can design, build the system, and explain what it does to a user or a city — including when the model should not decide. Domain plus AI is now common everywhere. The third language is the whole bet, and it only works if your studio section stays small enough that a tutor knows your work.

Best case for you

Trilingual Guild

You can design, code, and explain the user or city you just altered. Firms send problems because the section is small enough to use. Graduates are scarce and priced that way. Someone outside campus has a name for what you do.

Loved, small

Beloved Boutique

Everyone who knows SUTD loves it. Headcount is tiny. You will explain the university in every interview, because the national conversation still runs through NUS and NTU.

Grew the wrong way

Third Engineering School

To look bigger, intake rose. Studio hours fell. More classmates, less making. You are in a third engineering school with a design myth in the prospectus.

Worst case

Diluted Charter

“Design·AI” is on every page. Capstones are prompted decks. The founding MIT story is a museum. Employers cannot tell a SUTD graduate from any other AI-minor engineer.

Do this while you are here

  • Stay in a studio section small enough that the tutor knows your work. Protect those hours. That is the scarce resource.
  • Use the AI Gateway against itself: same brief, two models, write why you refused one.
  • Build one thing a user or an agency actually touched. Learn enough code to own it and enough design to defend it.
  • If your capstone is only slides, you used the wrong university. A portfolio beats a GPA here more than anywhere else in Singapore.
