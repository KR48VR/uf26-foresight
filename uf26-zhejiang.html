Board two-pager

For the President and University Party Committee

Crux

Use Hangzhou’s industrial density as apparatus — compute, data, firms, provincial cover — without letting AI STEP and the provincial highland turn ZJU into the training department of Zhejiang’s tech policy.

Diagnosis in six lines

ZJU is the C9 comprehensive that actually lives next to a commercial-technical civilization rather than only next to a political one. Alibaba-adjacent Hangzhou, a provincial government that talks about an AI industrial ecosystem, and a university that already runs a four-stage AI-in-education stack (STEP) and hosted the 2025 World University Presidents Forum — this is a tighter coupling than Beida or Fudan enjoy. Global ZJU 2.0 still talks like a cosmopolitan university. Dual circulation and the Institute of Global Development point the cosmopolitanism south and east, not toward the old Atlantic peer set. That can be a coherent strategy. It can also be a way of not noticing that the syllabus is being written in a provincial industrial plan.

Guiding policy

Take the cluster as an instrument. Keep problem choice and degree standards inside the university. Export AI-education method to the 78-university collaborative mechanism only if ZJU still examines the human, not the stack.

Coherent actions

  1. Publish an assessment standard for AI STEP: what a student must be able to do without the campus stack, or STEP is a vendor curriculum.
  2. Ring-fence a share of research that is not booked against provincial industrial tasks or firm roadmaps — and report it.
  3. Treat IGD as a Global South professional school with its own bar, not as a substitute for the research core on Zijingang.
  4. Keep a small number of Atlantic and East-Asian co-authorships alive as a legitimacy instrument, even when the political weather prefers South-South banners.
  5. Do not let “AI-empowered governance” become a second administration that bypasses faculty control of degrees.

Start

An assessment standard for AI STEP that a student must meet without the campus stack. A ring-fenced share of research not booked against firm roadmaps, reported.

Stop

STEP as a vendor curriculum. IGD as a substitute for the Zijingang research core. ‘AI-empowered governance’ as a second administration of degrees.

Go

Hangzhou adjacency. Provincial flagship plus a portable craft. Adapt the firm link; keep one unowned problem.

Full report

What ZJU is

A coastal comprehensive with unusual adjacency to private-sector scale. Hangzhou is not Beijing and not Shanghai. That difference is the strategy if the university can still say no to the nearest CEO and the nearest provincial bureau.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Provincial industrial policy as weather. Zhejiang is building an AI highland that explicitly fuses technology, industry, and education. ZJU is the obvious campus of that highland.
  • AI STEP as a whole-university operating system. Ma Yanming’s four-stage frame, plus a global collaborative mechanism of 78 universities launched at the November 2025 presidents’ forum in Hangzhou.
  • Global ZJU 2.0 vs. IGD. The former still sounds like a two-way cosmopolitan university. The latter, launched 15 March 2026 with Commerce and Education ministry cover, points the internationalism at the Global South and at Xi’s Global Development Initiative. First IGD graduate cohort: September 2026.
  • Talent and legitimacy under dual circulation. Official 2035 goal remains world-class with Chinese characteristics, including international faculty. The weather for Atlantic hires has not improved.
“It aims to promote AI-empowered education across the four stages to develop students not just as learners but as innovators.” — Ma Yanming on AI STEP, November 2025

Crux

Stay a university with a cluster attached, not a cluster with a university attached.

Kernel Reconstruction, 20 September 2026 — not Global ZJU 2.0

Diagnosis

ZJU’s location is the envy of every inland comprehensive and the risk that Beida does not have. Success looks like STEP metrics, provincial praise, and a South-facing institute. Failure looks the same from a distance, with thinner unowned science.

Guiding policy

Cluster as instrument. Degrees as university property. South-facing programs as an addition, not a replacement core.

Coherent actions

An examinable STEP standard. An unowned-research floor. IGD as a bounded professional school. A minimum international co-authorship hedge. Faculty control of degrees against “AI governance.”

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Who writes the problem list. University and faculty, or provincial-industrial tasking.

Axis B — Which abroad still counts. A two-way research commons, or a South-facing professional circuit with thin Atlantic legitimacy.

Faculty choice · Two-way

Hangzhou Commons

2030

AI STEP is real and examinable: a student must still complete a substantial piece with the campus stack off. Firms are on campus as instruments — named supervisors, killable projects — not as the syllabus. Co-authorships still travel to more than one system. The unowned-research floor is a published number, not a speech.

2040

The most industrially fluent comprehensive in China that other universities still treat as a peer, not a bureau. A hiring panel in Tokyo or Zurich can read a ZJU paper as science rather than as a provincial task report. Hangzhou’s cluster is attached to the university, not the other way around.

Faculty choice · South-facing

South Hub

2030

The Institute of Global Development is full. Atlantic ties thin. Quality inside the Zijingang core holds because faculty still own the exam. A student on an IGD track learns a region and a language, not only a model. The rest of the university is slightly more provincial than the brochure.

2040

A powerful Global South professional university with a Hangzhou research attic. Useful, funded, and less interchangeable with an old Atlantic peer. Dual circulation became a one-way circuit.

Tasking · Two-way

Provincial Flagship

2030

Rankings look fine. Syllabi read like industrial policy. Foreign partners stay for access to the cluster — Alibaba-adjacent data, provincial compute — rather than for the degree. A thesis that cannot be booked against a firm or a bureau is harder to supervise.

2040

A famous training arm of Zhejiang Inc. with international customers. STEP metrics are green. Unowned science is a memory the press office does not miss.

Tasking · South-facing

Utility

2030

STEP metrics, IGD headcount and provincial KPIs are all green. Unowned science is a memory. A student can complete a degree inside one vendor stack and one provincial task list. Faculty control of degrees has been bypassed by ‘AI-empowered governance.’

2040

An education-industry stack with a university logo. Other C9 campuses still call it ZJU. Hiring panels treat it as a highland training department.

Implications

Ma’s STEP language is usable if someone owns assessment. The provincial highland language is not a strategy; it is weather. IGD should be sized so that Zijingang does not become the leftover campus.
