Board two-pager

For the Dean and the Board

Crux

Keep the one-year, three-continent MBA as a judgment gym after AI cases and agent tools make content cheap — without the campuses drifting into three regional schools, and without HUMII becoming a content studio that forgot research.

Diagnosis in six lines

INSEAD is the business school that treated geography as curriculum. About 1,300 students sit in degree and PhD programmes; the MBA alone moves on the order of 1,100 people a year across two intakes and 90 nationalities. Singapore’s campus turned twenty-five in 2025 and now touches nearly 20,000 learners across programmes in a year. Veloso’s line is correct: the future is not human versus AI, it is judgment with machines in the room. The school has built the inventory — more than 40 AI-powered classroom experiences, immersive cases sold through INSEAD Publishing, HUMII launched in 2025 with a further €15 million gift, Global AI Forums across Abu Dhabi, Singapore, Fontainebleau and San Francisco. That is a product shop. The crux sits elsewhere. Visa regimes and polarised politics make campus hops expensive. Executive clients can buy AI cases without buying the year. Wharton and CEIBS exchanges are not a substitute for a school that still moves its own people. If Fontainebleau becomes Europe, Singapore becomes Asia, and Abu Dhabi becomes Gulf exec-ed, INSEAD has reproduced the industry it was founded not to be.

Guiding policy

Protect mobility as the degree. Use AI cases to make the room harder, not to replace it. Keep HUMII as research that changes what faculty teach, not as a second publishing house. Treat Singapore’s connector role as a geopolitical practice, not a brochure sentence.

Coherent actions

  1. A mobility floor: every MBA cohort still spends serious time on a second continent, with a published fallback if a visa corridor closes.
  2. Assessment in the room — live, multi-sided, with the AI protagonist and without it. Cases that only work as content are for the catalogue, not the degree.
  3. HUMII appointments inside departments, not beside them. The €15 million buys posts and doctoral seats, or it bought a logo.
  4. One intellectual agenda that can only be delivered because the school sits in Europe and Asia at once — supply chains, AI outside the US–China cartoon, family enterprise. Kill programmes that could be run from a single campus.

Start

One project that needs two campuses. A case protocol that treats an agent-finished memo as incomplete without a defended decision.

Stop

Online volume that turns the room into a feed. Treating three campuses as three photographs.

Go

The peer room. Multi-campus cohort. Adapt the case; keep the year that cannot be faked.

Full report

What INSEAD is

A one-year global MBA factory with a real multi-campus operating system, not a flagship-plus-satellites story. Faculty rotate. The same degree is awarded from France or Singapore. That architecture is the strategy. HUMII and immersive cases are how the school is trying not to be overtaken by its own content.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Cheap case content. If the case can be simulated, the year has to certify something the simulation cannot: judgment under disagreement, across rooms that do not share assumptions.
  • Visa and security politics. Hops are the product. Hops are also the first thing a nervous state cuts.
  • Polarisation as demand. Veloso has said the school is more useful when the world splits. True only if the classroom still mixes the split.
  • Singapore as connector. Sameer Hasija’s Asia-campus brief — hub, not replica. Twenty-five years is long enough to become local. Local is not the mission.
“The future of management education will not be about choosing between human expertise and AI, but about bringing the two together in ways that deepen learning and strengthen judgement.” — Francisco Veloso, September 2026

Crux

Keep the hops and the room as the scarce product while selling AI cases to the world. If the cases travel and the students do not, INSEAD has unbundled itself.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not INSEAD’s published plan

Diagnosis

INSEAD is a mobility school whose new AI inventory can either harden the year or replace it with a catalogue.

Guiding policy

Mobility floor. Harder rooms. HUMII as posts. One agenda that needs two continents.

Coherent actions

Published second-continent requirement and visa fallback. Live assessment with and without the agent. HUMII seats inside departments. Kill single-campus programmes that wear a global label.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Multi-campus mobility stays legally and culturally possible, or geopolitics freeze the hops.

Axis B — Human judgment in the room remains the product, or INSEAD becomes an AI-case publisher with a reunion brand.

A hops · B room

School for the world

2030

Every MBA cohort still spends a serious block on a second continent; the fallback if a visa corridor closes is published in the offer pack, not improvised in week six. Immersive AI cases make the room harder — students have to argue with and against the agent in front of classmates who do not share their priors. HUMII appointments sit inside strategy and organisational behaviour, not in a side building. Nationality concentration in any intake is treated as a product failure.

2040

Still the one-year global gym. Smaller perhaps, because hops cost more. Distinct, because the year still certifies judgment under disagreement rather than case consumption. A hiring partner can tell an INSEAD graduate from a person who bought the case catalogue.

A hops · B catalogue

Flying publisher

2030

People still travel. They consume the same AI cases they could have bought online through INSEAD Publishing. The hop is hospitality. Assessment is a write-up the agent can draft. HUMII produces events and inventory; syllabi in the core MBA move slowly.

2040

A hospitality layer on a content firm. The degree is a reunion brand plus a well-produced catalogue. Executive clients buy the cases without buying the year. Fontainebleau and Singapore photograph well.

A freeze · B room

Three rooms

2030

Campuses stop exchanging at scale: a visa corridor closes, or the school quietly regionalises to keep revenue. Each campus keeps a hard local classroom. An MBA can spend the year in Singapore and meet Europe only on a screen. Faculty rotation thins.

2040

A federation. The ‘world’ is a brand committee that meets quarterly. Three good regional schools share a logo and an alumni directory. The founding bet — one degree that moves — is a memory.

A freeze · B catalogue

Regional licences

2030

Both mobility and the room go. Each campus runs its own executive catalogue. The MBA shrinks into three local products with a shared crest. HUMII is a content studio attached to whichever campus still pays for it.

2040

Three executive schools sharing a logo. A hiring panel asks which campus you sat, then treats the answer as it would treat any regional MBA. The multi-continent year exists in the archive film.

Implications

A mobility floor and live assessment help in every world except “regional licences.” Selling cases without protecting hops pays the bills in “flying publisher” and spends the franchise.

A bad strategy would be: ship more immersive cases, let visa friction quietly regionalise the MBA, and call HUMII a success because the forum photographs well.

For students · INSEAD

The case bot will be fluent. The person across the table from another system will not be optional.

You came for a year that moves. Take the hop even when it is inconvenient. Treat every AI case as a warm-up for a room where the other side does not share your priors.

Best case for you

School for the world

You still hop. The room is harder on purpose: the person across the table does not share your priors. The network is a set of people who have seen you think, not a logo on LinkedIn.

Comfortable trap

Flying publisher

Great travel. Same cases as the catalogue, now drafted by the agent. Ask what you did in the room that a bot could not. If the answer is “I flew,” you used the wrong year.

Narrower, more serious

Three rooms

You got one campus deeply. The other two became optional. Build them yourself — a term, a team, a friendship that is professionally inconvenient — while the hop is still in the degree.

Worst case

Regional licences

You bought a logo. The world part was marketing. Keep a judgment trail that travels without the three-campus story.

Do this while you are here

  • Start Take the second continent. If your intake makes it optional, treat that as a warning.
  • Start Sit the case with the agent and without it. The gap is the education.
  • Start Build one friendship that is professionally inconvenient — different system, different industry.
  • Go Use HUMII and the Forums as instruments. Do not confuse attendance with judgment.
  • Go Keep a case you sat twice — once with the agent, once without. That gap is the education you can show later.
  • Stop Stop skipping the second continent because the hop is inconvenient. That hop is the product.
