Board two-pager

External reconstruction. Not a document that could be tabled, unedited, in Beijing.

Crux

Keep Peking a university whose humanities, social sciences and basic sciences still set questions the model and the ministry do not — while AI paper volume, a new chemist president, and a state that wants “AI + education” all pull Yanyuan toward becoming Tsinghua-with-a-lake.

Diagnosis in six lines

Peking’s historic advantage over Tsinghua was not compute. It was that a serious argument about law, history, economy or literature could still live on the same campus as a serious laboratory. That advantage is now the thing most at risk. In January 2026 CSRankings put PKU first in AI and computer vision; thirteen faculty published more than ten leading-conference papers each. That is a real achievement. It is also a gravitational field. Gao Song, a chemist who previously led South China University of Technology and Sun Yat-sen, arrived in May 2026 after Gong Qihuang. The Institute for Artificial Intelligence houses a centre for AI safety and governance. DI-IDEA, launched in 2023, now counts 36 universities including LSE and King’s. Those are instruments. They do not automatically preserve a campus where a non-STEM question can still set the agenda. Tsinghua is already the state’s education-AI utility. If Peking competes with Tsinghua on that job, it will lose, and it will have spent the one asset Tsinghua cannot copy.

Guiding policy

Treat AI paper leadership as a department fact, not the university strategy. Spend presidential attention on three things that do not scale by conference count: which non-STEM programmes still examine unassisted argument; which international alliances still move people rather than communiqués; and whether Gao’s first two years change promotion criteria in the humanities the way they will inevitably change them in chemistry and CS.

Coherent actions

  1. Publish a short list of programmes — law, history, economics, literature, basic maths, philosophy — where a binding assessment is still an unassisted human argument or proof. Count them annually.
  2. Keep DI-IDEA as a people-moving alliance, not a branding layer. A year with no reciprocal faculty or doctoral traffic is a press release.
  3. Do not clone Tsinghua’s knowledge-engine franchise. Peking’s scarce export is questions and methods, not course packs.
  4. Write a two-facing co-authorship rule that covers the humanities and social sciences, not only CS. A PKU historian who cannot be hired in Oxford is a warning, not a footnote.

Full report

What Peking is

Peking University is the state’s other flagship: older as a modern university, stronger in humanities and social sciences, and for a century the place where China’s elite argumentative culture was supposed to live. The Weiming Lake campus is the symbol. The Institute for Artificial Intelligence and the new CSRankings crown are the 2026 fact. Gao Song’s appointment on 23 May 2026 is the governance fact. A chemist president is not a problem if the humanities still own their exams. It is a problem if the only language the centre now speaks is paper volume and AI literacy.

PKU has spent the mid-2020s building the apparatus of an AI university: a safety-and-governance centre inside the AI institute; DI-IDEA as a China–Europe digital-intelligence alliance; public language about AI ethics as a trust strategy. At the 2026 World AI Conference season it talked joint platforms, fundamental theory, and people-centred governance. That is the official map. The unstated constraint is that Tsinghua already occupies the national education-AI utility role, and Peking will not beat Tsinghua at being Tsinghua.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • The Tsinghua comparison is the strategy problem. Every ministry, ranking house and parent already runs it. Competing on engines and course volume is how Peking loses its difference.
  • AI paper gravity. First in AI and CV on CSRankings 2026 is real. It will pull promotions, space, and undergraduate attention unless the centre sets a counterweight.
  • A new president with a STEM biography. Gao is a serious chemist and a veteran of two other presidencies. His first budget and his first promotion cycle will tell the humanities whether they are still a core or a decoration.
  • Alliance politics. DI-IDEA, Oxford, Cambridge, Max Planck, École Polytechnique. These only matter if people move. Communiqués do not keep a two-facing campus.
  • The state’s AI+ brief. The same Action Plan that turned Tsinghua into a utility will arrive on this campus as a demand to “AI-enable” the humanities. Enable is not the same as examine.
Peking University attaches high importance to AI ethics. We have established a centre for AI safety and governance within the Institute for Artificial Intelligence. — PKU public line, China–Europe AI cooperation, September 2026

Crux

The addressable difficulty is this: stop AI paper leadership and a chemist presidency from redefining Peking as a second national STEM flagship, and instead use both as instruments for a campus that still certifies unassisted argument. A new ethics centre does not touch it. A published list of model-forbidden assessments in the old faculties, and a promotion cycle that does not import CS counts into history, do.

Kernel Reconstruction, September 2026 — not Peking’s published plan

Diagnosis

Peking solved “look ancient and score modern.” The remaining problem is product definition: a comprehensive flagship whose only scarce difference from Tsinghua is the argumentative faculties, under a state and a ranking system that reward the opposite.

Guiding policy

Protect unassisted argument as the brand. Let CS win papers. Do not let CS set the exam for law or history. Keep alliances as people-moving instruments. Refuse the knowledge-engine franchise Tsinghua already owns.

Coherent actions

Annual count of model-forbidden assessments in the old faculties. DI-IDEA traffic metric. No Tsinghua-style course-pack export. Two-facing co-authorship rule that covers non-STEM. First Gao-era promotion cycle treated as a strategy document.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — International argumentative legibility. A PKU degree and a PKU paper in history, law or economics still clear in more than one system, or they become a parallel literature.

Axis B — What the campus is for. Yanyuan remains a university of argument plus science, or it becomes a STEM flagship with a heritage frontage.

A open · B argument

Weiming republic

2030

Law, history, economics and literature still set at least one binding assessment a model cannot sit — an unseen argument, a viva, a proof. CS keeps winning papers and does not set the promotion file for those faculties. DI-IDEA moves people: a year with reciprocal doctoral traffic, not only a membership list of 36 logos. Gao’s first promotion cycle leaves the humanities criteria intact.

2040

Peking is the two-facing comprehensive the state cannot replace with an engineering campus. The lake is not the product. The seminar is. A hiring panel in Oxford or Tokyo can still read a PKU historian or economist as a peer, not as a parallel literature.

A open · B STEM

Heritage frontage

2030

International traffic concentrates in CS and chemistry. The old faculties keep the buildings and lose the exam. A literature student can complete a degree as a model-fluent portfolio plus a campus tour. Visiting partners take the labs and skip the humanities seminar.

2040

Foreign partners take the labs and ignore the transcript in humanities. Yanyuan is a beautiful Tsinghua. The argument campus survives as architecture. Parents still want the name; employers in a second system ask which department.

A tight · B argument

National academy

2030

One bloc thins. Co-authorship and visiting traffic outside CS fall. The degree is scarce because the questions are local and the exams are still human — a PKU law paper still has to be argued in the room. A student who wanted a two-passport career starts arranging the second door early.

2040

Excellent and less internationally legible. The argument campus survives as a Chinese institution, not a two-facing one. Tsinghua remains the utility; Peking remains the academy. Foreign faculties treat both as counterparts rather than as peers who send and receive people.

A tight · B STEM

Second utility

2030

Both difference and openness go. Peking administers national AI-education modules with a lake on the brochure. Humanities assessments converge on the same literacy badges Tsinghua already ships. DI-IDEA is a communiqué machine.

2040

Research concentrates in a few institutes. The university is a hospitality layer over a science-and-cadres agency. The Weiming photograph remains. The seminar that made the photograph mean something does not.

Implications

Gao does not need to pick a world. He needs a first-two-years policy that is not ruined by three of the four. Model-forbidden assessments in the old faculties help everywhere except “second utility.” DI-IDEA helps in “Weiming republic.” It is wasted motion in “heritage frontage” unless people actually move.

A bad strategy would be: announce another AI ethics centre, chase Tsinghua’s course-pack KPI, leave the history exam as it was in 2019 with a literacy paragraph, and let CSRankings do the rest.

For students · Peking University

The lake will be on the prospectus. It will not write your argument.

You were admitted to the campus that was supposed to be able to host a fight Tsinghua would not. That is only true if your programme still marks an unassisted page. Choose the seminar, not the ranking screenshot.

Best case for you

Weiming republic

Harder seminars. Real vivas. A transcript that still sorts you from a prompt notebook.

Comfortable trap

Heritage frontage

CS eats the attention. Your humanities degree is a building tour. Employers start asking what you did with the camera off.

Narrow excellence

National academy

Fewer overseas doors. Deeper domestic ones. If you wanted a two-passport career, plan the second door yourself.

Worst case

Second utility

You collected a brand. The scarce jobs went to people who built things outside Yanyuan.

Do this while you are here

  • Collect at least one unassisted argument or proof per term. If your course has none, that is information about the course.
  • Use the AI institute as an instrument, not as a substitute for a department that still marks hard.
  • Watch the first Gao-era promotion cycle in your faculty. That is the real strategy document.
  • Keep a working relationship in a second system. Peking’s value was two-facing. So is yours.
