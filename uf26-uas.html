Board two-pager

For the Vice-Chancellor and Board

Crux

Build three shared UAS things the founding colleges cannot veto — a joint research platform, a shared graduate school, and a public studio calendar branded UAS first — or accept that the university title is a funding vehicle.

Diagnosis in six lines

UAS was announced in 2021, took students in 2024, and graduated 135 bachelor students in August 2026 (102 LASALLE, 33 NAFA). Intake is about 760 a year, aimed at 1,000. LASALLE and NAFA remain separate legal entities. Venka Purushothaman became LASALLE’s first Singapore-born president in February 2026. Tan-Soh Wai Lan runs NAFA. Chairman Lito Camacho. The alliance preserved two heritages and unlocked degree-awarding status. That form now blocks the thing the Board was sold: a national arts university that can talk to NUS CDE, SUTD Design·AI, and the creative economy as one counterpart.

Guiding policy

Do not wait for a merger that may never come. Force shared appointments, a graduate school, and one commencement identity while the first alumni cohort is still small enough to name.

Coherent actions

  1. Launch a UAS graduate school with joint supervision by 2028.
  2. Create one research centre the two colleges must staff together — practice-as-research, creative economy, AI and making.
  3. Give students a real cross-college semester, not a prospectus promise.
  4. Hold a single commencement identity. 2026 still read as two streams.
  5. Hire a small number of UAS-level artist-scholars who do not belong to only one college.

Start

A UAS graduate school with joint supervision by 2028. One research centre both colleges must staff. A real cross-college semester. A single commencement identity.

Stop

Waiting for a merger that may never come. Two-stream ceremonies. Prospectus promises of “one university” with no shared appointments.

Go

Keep LASALLE and NAFA making. Force shared work while the first alumni cohort is still small enough to name.

Full report

What it is

UAS is a publicly funded private university of the arts sitting on an alliance of LASALLE and NAFA. Office at the National Design Centre. Campuses in the Bras Basah–Bugis arts district. Seventh Singapore university by title. Youngest in institutional fact. UOA on the original list is this university, not Adelaide or Auckland.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • AI in image, sound, text and design — existential for studio pedagogy.
  • Alliance governance that protects heritage and slows integration.
  • National creative-economy ambitions without a single arts-university counterpart.
  • Tiny graduate numbers versus RCA, Parsons, or even NUS CDE arts-and-design.
“To be a future-facing institution that is audacious in its imagination and courageous in its purpose.” — Venka Purushothaman, on LASALLE, 2026

Crux

The degree exists. The university is still two colleges sharing a stamp. That is the addressable difficulty.

University · craft holds

The Making School

2030

An external examiner can fail a student in the room. AI is used as a tool and named as such. Joint graduate work is real; students move between McNally and Bencoolen as one body under one conferral standard. Graduates get work because a portfolio holds, not because the new title sounds grand. Practising makers are on contracts that assume a practice, not only a PhD.

2040

UAS is the regional school for people who still make things that have to stand up in front of other people. The alliance became a university without sanding off the two academies.

University · prompting

Creative-Economy Polytechnic

2030

Industry modules multiply; studio hours shrink. A prompted deck can pass as a final. Taught master’s exist as ranking plays with thin studio time.

2040

A well-branded supplier of content labour. The university title did not change the week.

Stamp · craft holds

Two Academies

2030

LASALLE and NAFA remain the real units. UAS is on the letterhead. Standards diverge in the room even when the crest does not. Applicants still pick a campus, not a university.

2040

Two art schools with a shared marketing office. Employers learn which campus to trust and ignore the rest.

Stamp · prompting

Brand Layer

2030

The title is used to recruit internationally. Standards diverge. Studio craft is uneven; prompting is unmarked. A student can complete a degree as a generative portfolio plus a campus brand.

2040

A brand wrapped around uneven training. Employers learn which campus to trust and ignore the rest.

Implications

Camacho’s board should force shared appointments and a graduate school while the first alumni cohort is still small enough to name. Waiting for organic integration is how alliances stay alliances.

A bad strategy would be: grow intake to 1,000, keep two HR systems, and call the stamp a university.

For students · UAS

You are the first classes. Act like founders.

University of the Arts Singapore is a new title over two older art schools, LASALLE and NAFA. Your degree says UAS. Your week still says one campus. The bet is that studio craft — work judged in the room, by hand and eye — still matters when prompting is cheap. If nobody can fail you standing up, the standard is not real yet.

Best case for you

Making School

An external examiner can fail a student in the room. AI is used as a tool and named as such. You leave with a house (LASALLE or NAFA) and a university. Graduates get work because a portfolio holds, not because the new title sounds grand.

Soft

Creative-Economy Polytechnic

Industry modules multiply. Studio hours shrink. Work is quick. Craft is thin. You become content labour with a better graphic on the certificate.

Old world

Two Academies

Your college is strong. UAS is paper — a shared marketing office. Applicants still pick a campus, not a university. Steal from the other house anyway.

Worst case

Brand Layer

The title is used to recruit internationally. Standards diverge. Employers learn which campus to trust and ignore the rest. Neither craft nor institution.

Do this while you are here

  • Start Pick a campus and a studio tutor, not a logo. Put work on the wall every term and defend it standing up.
  • Start Cross the other college this year, officially or not. Use AI in the process if you want; do not submit it as the work.
  • Start Treat the first alumni network as yours to invent. Ask who your supervisor reports to. If the answer is only one college, push for a second reader.
  • Start If nobody fails anyone in your year, the standard is not real yet. That is information. Act on it.
  • Stop Stop graduating as if you attended only one college. Cross the other shop while you can.
