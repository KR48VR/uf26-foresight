Board two-pager

For the President and University Party Committee

Crux

Make medicine–engineering fusion a faculty-governed production system — shared data, joint degrees, examinable clinical-AI judgment — rather than a string of named institutes whose problem list is set by a municipal industrial plan and a corporate co-builder.

Diagnosis in six lines

SJTU’s distinctive bet is not “AI” and not “a comprehensive university in Shanghai.” It is the short institutional distance between a first-rank engineering/CS bench and a first-rank medical school with a hospital system. Pudong campus (September 2025) and the June 2025 Medical AI Institute / AI hospital with WeDoctor are the physical form of that bet. Ding’s THE/Huawei remarks recast the teacher as project mentor. That is the right sentence if the project is chosen inside the university. It is the wrong sentence if the project is a product milestone in someone else’s hospital-AI stack. Shanghai municipal policy will keep paying for the second version unless SJTU writes the first.

Guiding policy

Own the joint degree and the clinical-AI exam. Treat firms and districts as sites. Keep a basic-science floor that cannot be booked as a hospital product. Do not let Minhang and Pudong become two universities that share a badge.

Coherent actions

  1. Launch a single medicine–engineering doctoral pathway with a shared exam, not a pair of co-branded certificates.
  2. Put Institute and AI-hospital data under a university governance rule: what can be trained, what must be withheld, who can publish.
  3. Cap the fraction of medical-AI faculty time billable to the corporate co-builder or district tasking.
  4. Keep Minhang basic CS/engineering as a peer of the medical build-out, with hiring quotas that prevent Pudong from eating the bench.
  5. Require every flagship “AI + Medicine” demo to name the clinical failure mode it still cannot handle — or it is a launch event.

Full report

What SJTU is

A Shanghai engineering university that grew a medical empire and now wants the two to be one field. The 130-year-old brand still carries a Jiaotong mobility-and-industry memory. The live bet is clinical AI at city scale.

Forces that actually move this institution

  • Medical-AI vertical. 27 June 2025: SJTU, the School of Medicine, and WeDoctor agree to co-build a Medical AI Institute (Fan Xianqun as dean) and an AI hospital. Stated aim: a closed loop from demand to original technique to clinic to industry.
  • Pudong as a second medical geography. School of Medicine Pudong campus opened 12 September 2025; multi-campus map now includes Huangpu, Pudong, Hainan, Chongming, plus a planned “Medical Valley” translation park drawing 13 affiliated hospitals.
  • A ranking claim used as strategy. The university cites CSRankings AI at or near global first in 2025. Useful for recruiting. Dangerous as a substitute for a kernel.
  • Pedagogy talk from the president. Ding at THE Connect with Huawei, 2026: teachers as project mentors; students using AI on global problems; “innovation is driven by people.” The sentence has to be operationalized as an exam, or it is a keynote.
  • Municipal and national health-industrial policy. Healthy China, Shanghai biomedical highland, district governments that want a park. Weather, not strategy.
“Innovation is driven by people. By building strong platforms and attracting global talents, we create an ecosystem where great minds and great ideas inspire each other.” — Ding Kuiling, THE Connect, 2026

Crux

Govern the medicine–machine joint as a university — shared exam, shared data rule, shared bench — before Shanghai and a corporate co-builder govern it as a product line.

Diagnosis

SJTU has the two ingredients other universities try to buy separately. Putting them in adjacent buildings is not fusion. Fusion is a degree and a data rule. Right now the visible artifacts are buildings, an institute, and a partner.

Guiding policy

Own the joint credential and the failure modes. Firms are sites. Minhang stays a peer campus, not a feeder.

Coherent actions

One joint doctoral pathway. A university data-governance rule for the AI hospital. A cap on billable faculty time. Hiring quotas that protect Minhang. Every demo names a failure mode.

Critical uncertainties

Axis A — Who owns the clinical-AI agenda. Faculty-governed fusion, or municipal-corporate product line.

Axis B — Where the bench lives. Minhang and Pudong remain one university, or the medical vertical becomes the institution.

Medical Valley, Governed

2030

A single medicine–engineering doctoral pathway exists, with a shared exam rather than two co-branded certificates. The AI hospital publishes failure modes — what the system still cannot safely do — not only launch demos. Minhang CS still hires stars; Pudong has not eaten the bench. 2040

The reference design for clinical AI that is still a university. A foreign medical school can read an SJTU joint doctorate as science plus clinic, not as a vendor internship. Shanghai got a highland. Jiaotong kept the question.

Faculty fusion · Vertical wins

Hospital University

2030

Medicine-AI is superb. Minhang is a feeder. The comprehensive claim thins: basic engineering hires slow while Pudong grows. A student who wanted a portable CS craft has to protect it themselves. Affiliated hospitals set more of the weekly rhythm than departments do.

2040

A world-class medical-AI institution that used to be Jiaotong. The 130-year mobility-and-industry memory is a museum label. Employers in general engineering hire from Minhang’s remainder or from ZJU.

Product line · One university

Park Campus

2030

Demos ship. Degrees look the same as 2024. Foreign partners come for access to the hospital stack and the district park, not for a joint exam. Faculty time billable to the co-builder creeps up. A capstone can be completed inside one product milestone.

2040

A well-ranked municipal product organisation with students. The brand still opens a first meeting. The second meeting is with WeDoctor or the district, not with a department.

Product line · Vertical wins

WeDoctor College

2030

The partner and the district set the weekly rhythm. Faculty publish less and launch more. Data rules are the co-builder’s. A student learns a stack they cannot take with them.

2040

A brand licensed onto a health-industrial park. The university stationery remains. Problem choice does not.

Implications

Ding’s people-first sentence is the right policy only if the next budget protects unowned CS and a joint exam. The AI hospital is a gift with a governance problem inside it. Pudong should not be allowed to become the whole story.

For students · SJTU

If your project only works inside one hospital’s stack, you did not learn fusion. You learned a vendor.

Minhang and Pudong will both claim you. Cross them on purpose.

Governed Valley

Medicine wins

Hospital University

Park logic

Park Campus

Great facilities, assigned problems. Keep one question with no product owner.

Worst case

WeDoctor College

The stack is the curriculum. Build a portable craft and a second passport of methods.

Do this while you are here

  • Take one course or rotation on the campus that is not your home campus.
  • If you work on medical AI, write down what the model must not be allowed to decide.
  • Keep a basic-science collaborator in Minhang even if your badge says Pudong.
  • Treat launch events as warnings, not as the work.
